# Product Idea Compass

Language for developing a reusable mentor room that helps explore and evaluate software ideas.

## Language

**Mentor room**:
The reusable, agent-guided environment in which Brandon develops and evaluates software ideas using the selected experts' published methods.

**Idea**:
A proposed software product or change to existing software, at any size or stage of development.

**Component idea**:
A smaller idea within a larger proposition that may have promise independently or in a different combination or context.

**Idea case**:
The separate history of exploring one idea, including its component ideas, artifacts, evidence, and decisions.

**Mentor session**:
A back-and-forth conversation applying a selected expert's methods to an idea and identifying useful exercises, artifacts, or next actions.

**Playbook**:
A source-grounded account of an expert's method, including the activities and outputs needed to apply it.

**Artifact**:
A concrete output of developing or testing an idea, such as a customer profile, hypothesis map, prototype, or test record.

**Mandatory core**:
The Knapp–Zeratsky work every idea must account for; existing work can satisfy an activity when it meets the method's requirements, with gaps filled.

**Coverage**:
The record of which experts and activities have been considered or completed for an idea, including reasons for omitted sessions.

**Core baseline**:
A versioned set of mandatory Knapp–Zeratsky activities and completion checks tied to the sources actually reviewed.

**Evidence record**:
A dated observation or source claim with its provenance, audience, context, and limitations, distinct from the interpretation drawn from it.

**Investment decision**:
A recommendation about the next bounded commitment to an idea, judged against that idea's intended benefit and remaining uncertainty.

**Stopped—core incomplete**:
A case the user has elected to end while required core work remains unfinished; its findings, components, and coverage history remain available.
