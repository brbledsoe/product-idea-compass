## What this research changes

The broad proposition has substantial overlap with existing products. A cleaner-backup workflow also has direct competition. An opportunity may remain in a particular region, provider workflow, onboarding experience, or degree of responsibility, but none has been established here.

Published features cannot reveal whether a host can complete setup, whether a provider accepts a last-minute job, whether repairs are done well, or whether an owner feels able to stop checking. Those require observed workflows and actual local participation.

Next competitor work should replay one concrete family incident through the relevant existing products. Record what is already solved, what requires configuration, what requires a provider relationship, and what still needs a human. No account creation, trial subscription, external messaging, or booking was performed in this session.
