## Interim recommendation

Insufficient evidence to assess business viability or fund a broad agency-replacement build. Recommend a bounded learning investment: reconstruct the recent incident, identify reachable hosts and current alternatives, then choose the smallest discriminating test. This is facilitator advice; the founders own the investment decision.

Evidence that would change the recommendation: repeated costly failures in reachable non-family hosts, observed advantage over actual alternatives, willingness to pay/continue, viable integration permissions, dependable local provider participation, and acceptable support costs. Evidence that current tools solve the incident cheaply would favor using those tools or changing the proposition.
