# Idea development sessions

These facilitation sequences are our adaptations of the identified source practices. Use a session only when its question is relevant. Preserve the mandatory core and reuse its customer, competition, and option artifacts after checking fit.

## Pincus

Read [idea methods](../../../../research/mentor-room/pincus-idea-methods.md) for exact EPUB anchors and [iteration and fit](../../../../research/mentor-room/pincus-iteration-and-fit.md) when moving beyond the first concept test.

**Use for:** attachment to an implementation, a bundle of partly promising ideas, uncertain novelty, oversized prototypes, or initial interest without retention. Prerequisites: a provisional human need and the intended audience; if either is unclear, begin with recent real experiences rather than a pitch.

1. Separate the underlying need/instinct, the current whole-product configuration, and atomic components. Ask what would remain worth solving if this implementation disappeared. Generate plausible variants without declaring the underlying instinct immune to failure. Output: a component and alternative map (`PINCUS-INSTINCT-VARIANTS`).
2. Examine actual products this audience uses. Ask which exact mechanics are Proven, what customers demonstrably consider Better, and which changes remain New. Maker conviction alone does not establish Better. Output: a PBN table with source/evidence and unresolved classifications (`PINCUS-PBN-*`).
3. Identify the smallest idea that could fail independently. Ask what a person would have to experience for that uncertainty to become observable, and where those people can be reached. Output: an atomic test target and access plan (`PINCUS-TEST-ACCESS`, `PINCUS-MIS`). MIS means **Minimum Idea State**.
4. Compare a manual experience, a realistic walkthrough/ripomatic, and a tiny usable slice. Select the one that exposes the target uncertainty at acceptable effort. Mark simulated functionality honestly. Output: an MIS brief/prototype and experiment contract; an attractive demo alone is not evidence (`PINCUS-RIPOMATIC`).
5. Review results by behavior: attraction, trying, repeat use, and durable value. Ask whether familiar mechanics caused the positive result while the New element contributed nothing. Preserve losers and surprises, then choose a variant, further test, or stop (`PINCUS-SIGNAL-REVIEW`).

**Session completion:** the targeted component, familiar baseline, novel change, audience, falsifying observation, and next test are explicit; after fieldwork, conclusions cite actual results. Ten-of-ten Better and the book's percentages are author heuristics, not universal sample rules or success probabilities. Friends' enthusiasm and AI suggestions do not settle customer demand. Broader personal-life exercises are optional when purpose is the uncertainty, not a mandatory startup gate.

## Pattern breakers

Read [opportunity methods](../../../../research/mentor-room/pattern-breakers-methods.md) for source tables/anchors and [evidence and fit](../../../../research/mentor-room/pattern-breakers-evidence-and-fit.md) for customer learning and later strategy.

**Use for:** a claimed inflection, non-obvious insight, timing advantage, or breakthrough ambition. A useful personal tool or incremental feature need not qualify as a pattern breaker. Prerequisites: the claimed change and its intended beneficiaries, even if provisional.

1. Separate outside-world inflection, behavioral insight, product implementation, and components (`PB-IDEA-LAYERS`). Ask which of these would actually be disproved by the current evidence. Output: a layered hypothesis map.
2. Apply the Inflections Stress Test: what specific event/threshold creates a new capability, who gains power, by how much, and which external conditions must hold? Record dated sources and unresolved conditions. “AI is growing” is not a specific mechanism (`PB-INFLECTION-TEST`).
3. Read the saved Insight Stress Test table before facilitating it. Examine the non-consensus belief, why the proposed insight could be right, what the status quo misses, and why this can become durable value. Give the skeptical alternative its strongest explanation. Output: an insight hypothesis and observations that would distinguish it from the alternative; dissent alone is not proof.
4. Ask what the founder has actually experienced with the new capability or pioneering customers. Choose a hands-on exposure task or narrow prototype when the account is only speculative (`PB-FUTURE-IMMERSION`, `PB-FIRSTHAND-PROBLEM`). Output: a learning task with a concrete observation target.
5. Examine surprises and unusually engaged users without generalizing from a convenient niche. Decide whether to change implementation, investigate an audience, or revisit the underlying insight. Keep evidence tied to the relevant layer.

**Session completion:** a specific enabling change, explicit insight, falsifiable conditions, and a practical learning action are documented. A compelling narrative, completed stress-test table, contrarian opinion, or retrospective success story is not market validation. Novelty is conditional on the user's ambition. Pincus may deliberately preserve familiar interaction mechanics while this lens tests a different basis of customer choice; these can coexist.
