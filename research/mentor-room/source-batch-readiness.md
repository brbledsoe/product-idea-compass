## The five books contribute different kinds of help

| Source group | Useful contribution to the room | Boundary to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| [Knapp–Zeratsky / Click and sprint sources](click-book-method.md) | Develop the customer/problem/approach hypothesis, compare alternatives, choose what to prototype, and learn from customer reactions. | The mandatory core comes from Brandon's instruction. A completed exercise or prototype interview does not establish every commercial or technical claim. |
| [Pincus / Life at the Speed of Play](pincus-idea-methods.md) | Separate an instinct from its implementations; isolate uncertain components; learn cheaply and iterate. | Familiar mechanics can obscure whether the new component has value. A promising component needs evidence in its own audience and configuration. |
| [Maples–Ziebelman / Pattern Breakers](pattern-breakers-methods.md) | Examine enabling changes, timing, distinct insights, and potential new behaviors; diagnose implementation versus audience versus insight. | Breakthrough-business criteria apply when that ambition is relevant. They do not disqualify a useful personal tool or incremental feature. |
| [Savoia / The Right It](the-right-it-methods.md) | Specify observable engagement, choose a small pretotype, and measure meaningful commitments before investing more. | Initial interest, attempted purchases, actual payments, and return behavior are separate observations; numerical heuristics are not calibrated success probabilities. |
| [Bland–Osterwalder / Testing Business Ideas](testing-business-ideas-process.md) | Map risky assumptions, choose relevant experiments from a broad library, record evidence and learning, and decide the next action. | Experiments answer particular questions. Discovery/validation labels and graphical evidence ratings do not certify an entire idea. |

These contributions can share artifacts. They should not cause the same interview, observation, or experiment to be counted as multiple independent pieces of evidence. The source-grounded [evidence notes](evidence-and-overlap.md) and each book's fit section record further distinctions.

## A concrete direction for the first conversation prototype

The user's established preferences suggest a room with a coordinating facilitator: accept an idea at any maturity, clarify what further investment must accomplish, inspect prior work and component ideas, then recommend a relevant next mentor session while keeping mandatory-core progress visible. Each session should leave an updated artifact or finding, evidence still needed, and a concrete next action. At major transitions, offer the next relevant expert or group and explain why.

The order can respond to the idea and the evidence. A session that discovers a more urgent unknown can redirect the next task; a partially useful idea can retain promising parts. Existing work earns credit only after checking it against the exercise's actual requirements. How long sessions should be, how strongly to challenge the user, and how incomplete or abandoned cases are recorded still need the facilitation prototype and the mandatory-core discussion.
