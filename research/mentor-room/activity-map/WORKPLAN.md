# Activity and prospective skill map

Requested 2026-09-09. This is a new research deliverable, not a revision to the mentor-room skill or its feedback archive. Original books, extracts, and existing case work stay in place.

## Intended result

A navigable catalog of recommended exercises, tasks, processes and deliverables in the supplied five books, related workshop materials, and the 49-resource inventory. Show substantive substeps, inputs and output fields, categories, precise source locations, source variants, dependencies and freedom to reorder. Propose possible skill interactions and sensible shared skills without writing or installing those skills.

Source instructions, dependency inferences, and suggested AI behavior must be distinguishable. Preserve unavailable source coverage explicitly; do not reconstruct missing books, transcripts, courses or tools from marketing descriptions. Fictional personas may support exploration only as labeled hypotheses.

## Requirements and completion evidence

- [ ] Source inventory: all five books, supplied workshop assets, and all 49 CSV rows accounted for, with access/review limits.
- [ ] Primary-source review: book sections and available resource sections checked for actions beyond prior method notes; coverage ledger records included activities and justified exclusions.
- [ ] Activity catalog: stable IDs, purpose, concrete steps/subitems, inputs, meaningful output fields, original source reference, kind and category.
- [ ] Order map: source workflow order separate from real hard inputs, recommended predecessors, independent entry points, parallel/repeat opportunities; each proposed dependency has a reason and basis.
- [ ] Skill sketches: AI vs user/fieldwork roles, likely interaction and deliverable; clearly proposals, not authored skill files.
- [ ] Merge map: shared output/contracts, related activities, differences to preserve, candidates that should remain separate, and example compositions.
- [ ] User-profile example: concrete hypothetical interaction, fictional/evidenced labels, and a route to replace guesses with observations.
- [ ] Reviewable artifacts: human-readable guide, complete structured catalog and coverage data, browsable explorer, and concise entry point.
- [ ] Verification: referential integrity, all expected experiment/board items, source links and coverage, category/execution/dependency fields, substantive manual spot checks, explorer operation and responsive layout.
