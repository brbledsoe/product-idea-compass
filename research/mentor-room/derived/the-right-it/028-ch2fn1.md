<span id="rch2f1"></span>[*](010-chapter-2.md#ch2f1)   The problems that make Thoughtland-based market research so undependable for predicting market interest in a product belong to a family of cognitive errors and biases that are themselves the subject of much research. The book <em>Thinking, Fast and Slow</em> by Daniel Kahneman (New York: Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2011) is a great resource if you need more evidence and examples of how our seemingly rational brain can fool most of us most of the time.
