## Question

Across the core discovery and validation sources in the CSV, what is each method for, what evidence does it require, and which outputs can legitimately be reused across methods? What differences must survive the synthesis, especially for raw ideas versus existing features and usability evidence versus demand or product-market fit?

## Answer

2026-09-05 — Resolved by primary-source research in [Evidence methods that can share work without sharing conclusions](../../../research/mentor-room/evidence-and-overlap.md).

The verified methods serve distinct questions: customer experiences, observable demand, experiment design, opportunities and alternatives, four product risks, staged investment, and PMF among experienced users. Work can be reused when its claim, population, context, and quality fit the next method; reuse does not create additional independent evidence. Venture and bootstrap screens assume different ambitions. Component-level observations must retain their original context when the overall idea changes.

This is a bounded comparison of public sources, not an exhaustive operational playbook for every method. Proposed synthesis is labeled separately from source claims. Authoring and testing the combined workflow remain open.
