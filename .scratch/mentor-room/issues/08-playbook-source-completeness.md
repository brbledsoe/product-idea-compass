## Question

What must a saved mentor playbook contain to guide an actual conversation and exercise reliably: source locations, prerequisites, activities, questions, outputs, completion checks, evidence limits, and adaptation notes? Using the initial audit, decide which resources need deeper reading or user-provided access before their methods can be offered as complete. Distinguish an accessible summary from a fully operational playbook.

## Answer

The first [skill](../../../.agents/skills/mentor-room/SKILL.md) routes to concise playbooks with triggers, prerequisites, source pointers, operative questions/actions, artifacts, completion checks and inference limits. Full source locators remain in the research notes. [Routing](../../../.agents/skills/mentor-room/references/routing.md) accounts for every original catalog row and distinguishes ready material, public adaptations, partial dialogue, metadata, and unavailable curricula. Five supplied book groups and five supplementary public sessions are usable; other specialists receive focused primary-source research when needed. The source cap remains respected and no additional upload is a prerequisite. This is usable scoped coverage, not a claim that all 49 resources are completely acquired.
