## Question

What must each idea retain so a new session can distinguish assumptions, research, real observations, artifacts, contradictions, and decisions; reuse justified work; and select a concrete next action? How should uncertainty and conflicting recommendations affect a pursue, revise, stop, or insufficient-evidence conclusion?

## Answer

Under Brandon's 2026-09-06 delegation, [case rules](../../../.agents/skills/mentor-room/references/case-rules.md) define a pinned, separate case record with hypotheses, evidence provenance/context, experiments, artifact references, coverage, chronological sessions, append-only decisions, and concrete next actions. Interpretations remain separate from observations; matching work may be reused without multiplying evidence. Decisions address the next bounded investment under the case's own success criteria. Resumption starts with saved scope, latest decision, pending action and relevant artifacts. The initializer/checker enforces structural continuity while leaving methodological and truth checks to the facilitator.
