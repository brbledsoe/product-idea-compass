# Preserve promising component ideas when the whole changes

Type: grilling
Labels: wayfinder:grilling
Status: resolved
Assignee: coordinator
Parent: ../map.md
Blocked by: 04, 06

## Question

How should an idea case represent component ideas, distinguish evidence about a component from evidence about their combination, and preserve promising parts when the whole is revised or rejected? What makes a component a separate idea case, and which claims need retesting when its customer, purpose, or context changes?

## Comments

Brandon explicitly raised this requirement during the initial coverage conversation. Preserve component potential without silently concluding that a component has been validated as a standalone product.

## Answer

[Case rules](../../../.agents/skills/mentor-room/references/case-rules.md) keep components with proposition, role, hypotheses, evidence, original context, current assessment, and alternative contexts. A component stays parked within its parent until Brandon elects to explore it as its own proposition. A linked child then gets independent scope, criteria and coverage; inherited observations are prior evidence needing applicability review. A failed whole neither refutes nor validates every part. These mechanics implement Brandon's explicit component requirement under his delegated design authority.
