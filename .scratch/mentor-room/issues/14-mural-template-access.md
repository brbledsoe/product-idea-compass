## Answer

2026-09-05 — [Mural template access](../../../research/mentor-room/mural-template-access.md) verifies the exact supplied link and its public preview. The title is Foundation Sprint Template v1.1, attributed to Jonathan Vardy. Actual canvas content was partially inspected; the source is retained as a third-party implementation, distinct from the official guide and unattributed CSV.

No export was found in the public preview, and the browser's content-export operation was unsupported. Automatic approval review rejected the template-start click because it might create persistent account/board state; it was not executed or bypassed. The public resource is documented, but no editable copy, complete transcription, or local export was acquired. This resolves the access investigation, not an unperformed copy operation.
